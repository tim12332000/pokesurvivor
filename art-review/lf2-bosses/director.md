## Known traps to check early

- Classic sprite/dat semantics define identity; do not invent ability names or quietly use Remaster/modded kits.
- Body release time and effect spawn/impact time are separate. Sampling a wrong release frame can produce a correct-looking flash from the wrong socket.
- Original LF2 fullscreen finishers can become visually unreadable in a narrow top-down viewport; keep the player and reachable ground visible without removing legal damage.
- Quantity ×1.25 uses floor thresholds. Single-base attacks do not gain a whole actor on the first card; show accumulation rather than a false increase.
- Fired projectiles and released fields continue independently, while death/unlearn/world/epoch invalidation cancels future contact and asynchronous audio.
- The public pack includes ignored `.omx` modules only through explicit inventory. Test precommit packaging, not only local files.
- Existing static Pages source is a separate release branch; feature push does not update the public game. Preserve domain/protection settings.
