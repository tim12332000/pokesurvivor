## Original/adapted labels

Swift Pursuit, Ninja Tool Volley, Protective Formation, Ninja Tool Ricochet,
Ninja Tool Mastery, Returning Fuma Blades, Explosive Scroll: Scattering Blossoms,
Three-Star Fan Mastery, Typhoon Rotation, Returning Wind Wall, Quicksand Prison,
Shattering Counter, Black Receiver Volley, and the Bone Boomerang / Bone Wall
labels describe this project's adapted mechanics. They are not advertised as
verbatim English anime attack names. The English skill descriptions retain
the adaptation statement wherever the Chinese source supplies it.

## Verification limits

Accessible official text did not provide every minor attack's anime-dub line.
Willow Dance, Camellia Dance, Great Wind Scythe Jutsu, Tiger Lily and the
Vine/Flower subdivision are retained as recognizable English technique labels,
but this record does **not** certify their exact spelling against an anime audio
track. Other pre-existing English names remain conventional labels unless
specifically evidenced above. Do not describe this table as a full dub audit.

English releases vary: the official manga-oriented Lee column uses Forward Lotus,
Reverse Lotus, Gate of Closing and Gate of Joy, while the official anime pages
use Primary Lotus / Hidden Lotus. Preserve the selected anime labels instead of
blindly replacing them with another official release's terminology.
